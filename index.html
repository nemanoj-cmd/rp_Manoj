Effect of UV Light Radiation on Planarian Regeneration

Research Project

Background

Planarians are well known for their regenerative properties, allowing them to regrow parts of their body when harmed. This is due to the presence of collagen and stem cells, which allow for rapid regeneration rates. This study looks at how long-term exposure to ultraviolet (UV) light can affect regenerative processes, regarding survival rates and movement.

Rationale

Similarly to humans, Planarians utilize collagen to regulate the creation of these cells and play a large part in the regeneration process. Because of these features, planarians are often used to model cell growth in other organisms, including humans.
